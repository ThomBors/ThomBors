## Research Interests

My research focuses on **Multi-Task Learning (MTL)** and **Deep Neural Networks**, with particular emphasis on **transfer learning** and **explainable AI**.

Most Machine Learning (ML) models are trained to solve a single task and are difficult to adapt to others. In contrast, **Multi-Task Learning (MTL)** enables a model to learn multiple tasks simultaneously by exploiting shared representations.

This learning paradigm resembles human learning processes, where general skills improve through exposure to multiple tasks and can be reused across different contexts.

Recent studies show that MTL can lead to better-generalised models by leveraging both similarities and differences among tasks.  
In my PhD research, I investigate **novel MTL approaches for Deep Neural Networks**, aiming to:

- contribute to the **theory of transfer learning in MTL**
- improve **model explainability**
- enhance **monitoring and optimisation strategies** for effective MTL deployment
